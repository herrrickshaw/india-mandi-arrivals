# Data licence

The code in this repository is MIT-licensed (see `LICENSE`).

The data under `data/` is re-published from Government of India sources:

- **Agmarknet** (Directorate of Marketing & Inspection, Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare), https://agmarknet.gov.in/
- **UPAG** (Unified Portal for Agricultural Statistics, Department of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare), https://upag.gov.in/

Both are published by the Government of India. Re-use here follows the
[Government Open Data License – India (GODL)](https://www.data.gov.in/Godl): attribute the source,
don't misrepresent the data, and don't imply endorsement. The files are an unofficial,
point-in-time extract. Check figures against the source portals before relying on them.

Market directory addresses are institutional (market yards). Fields that name individual
officials or carry phone numbers or email addresses are dropped by the collectors.
